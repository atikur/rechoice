Optional / Try-on preview

Choose who the preview shows.

To draw the two tops on a person, Rechoice sends that person's photo and the product photos to YouCam, an AI try-on service.

  • YouCam keeps uploaded and generated images for up to 30 days, stores them in the USA, and says it does not use them to train models.
  • Rechoice deletes its own copies within 24 hours. Deleting a photo here does not delete YouCam's copy.
  • A preview is approximate. It cannot show fit, exact color, drape or fabric feel.
Who should the preview show?

{% for model in demo_models %}{% endfor %}

Demo models are AI-generated images from Pixabay. They show no real person.

For a usable preview

  • One adult, standing and facing the camera.
  • Chest up, with both shoulders in the photo.
  • The whole face visible, with no hand, hair or sunglasses covering it.
  • The person fills most of the frame, ideally against a plain background.
  • A JPEG or PNG at least 512 by 384 pixels and at most 9.5 MB.

Recorded results cover the demo people only, so your own photo uses a live preview.
