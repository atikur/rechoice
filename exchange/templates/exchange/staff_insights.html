What it could mean for a store: a worked example

An illustration from published figures and your own inputs, not a forecast. The figures are averages from other stores; a pilot would measure yours.

  1. 19.3% of online sales are expected to be returned in 2025: NRF and Happy Returns, 2025 Retail Returns Landscape, 15 October 2025.
  2. 28% of shoppers who return apparel do so because it didn't look the way they thought it would: PowerReviews, survey of 25,452 US consumers, June 2023. This is apparel; other categories will differ.
  3. In the US, 17.1% of returns become exchanges and 76.1% end in refunds: Loop Returns, 2025 State of Ecommerce Returns, 13.8 million returns from 4,000+ Shopify merchants.
