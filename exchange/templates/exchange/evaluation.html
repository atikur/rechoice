What can we trust in a preview?

Rechoice checks narrow properties of generated images and keeps them separate from the store's product facts. This page reruns the fidelity gate and the other checks on every published render, including two registered gate studies and the rejected bag probe.

The fidelity gate: does the preview show what was asked for?

For top previews with the required inputs, the gate makes three local measurements, with no model call: garment colour against the catalog (ΔE ≤ {{ gate.thresholds.colour_delta }}), print busyness against the top bought on the same photo (quieter at ≤ {{ gate.thresholds.quiet_ratio }}× its edge detail), and the share of the image outside a fixed garment band that changed (stable at ≤ {{ gate.thresholds.stable_pct }}%). A result outside these thresholds is flagged, and the shopper must choose it knowingly. These measurements do not establish overall fidelity.

Registered study v2: four new people, an attempt to make the gate fail, and repeats

Registered before any render (report, protocol assets/gate_study_v2.json): each of {{ v.people|length }} people the gate had never measured got the floral blouse, five plain long-sleeved tops and two patterned sweaters registered as tops that should not pass. The three v1 colour flags were rendered twice more on the same photos. Thresholds were not changed after the results; a blind AI rater labelled every render.

{{ v.busy_not_shows.n }} / {{ v.busy_not_shows.of }}patterned sweaters the gate did not pass (catch rate)
{{ v.plain_print_quieter.n }} / {{ v.plain_print_quieter.of }}plain tops measured quieter than the blouse
{{ v.plain_colour_close.n }} / {{ v.plain_colour_close.of }}plain-top colours within ΔE {{ gate.thresholds.colour_delta }} of the catalog
{{ v.renders_stable.n }} / {{ v.renders_stable.of }}renders stable outside the garment band
{{ v.plain_shows.n }} / {{ v.plain_shows.of }}plain tops where all three measurements agree with the request

What held. Each repeat reproduced its v1 colour flag within 0.2 ΔE, across all six repeats of three flagged cases; these retries did not remove the colour flags. On the three half-body photos all 24 renders stayed within the outside-band threshold.

What did not. The four plain tops not measured quieter were all the utility shirt, whose pockets, buttons and seams the print measure reads as pattern. The sweaters were weak negatives: a yoke band and broad stripes are less busy than the dense floral, the blind rater judged all eight quieter, and the gate passed six of them on print. On the full-length photo the stability band flagged seven of eight renders at 3.0 to 4.5 percent; the rater confirmed redrawn trousers or sleeves in three. The rater also saw small changes inside the garment band, such as sunglasses hung on a shirt, that the fixed band cannot see. On colour the rater accepted all ten flagged renders, allowing for the photo's light; the colour check does not correct for it.

What each check establishes

QuestionEvidence availableLimit
Does the replacement meet the request?Catalog eligibility checks stock, policy, budget and confirmed controls.It does not prove that the image shows those facts faithfully.
Is the product detail recognizable?{{ s.prior_ai_reviews }} prior clothes reviews, attributed to the AI assistant, plus narrow colour and relative-size measurements.No new independent human review; pattern, construction and physical fit are not automatically verified.
Is the requested difference visible?A separate rubric field for a reviewer to score.{{ s.requested_difference_reviewed }} rated so far. A pilot task is not evidence of a shopper's confirmed request.
Did unrelated parts stay unchanged?Outside-region pixel change is reported for each comparable image.Fixed approximate boxes and compression can affect it. This historical development measurement is exploratory. The gate above uses a separate 3% threshold; neither verifies identity, pose or background preservation.
Are misleading results handled?The colour warning, earring correction and the rejected bag probe are visible below. The rejected necklace is described in pilot evidence.These are known development examples, not a measured failure-detection sensitivity.

Review protocol and separate photo holdout

Score product detail as recognizable, partly changed, misleading or not assessed; requested difference as visible, not visible, not applicable or not assessed; unrelated parts as preserved, changed or not assessed; and misleading result as yes, no or not assessed.

python manage.py evaluate_previews --review-template /tmp/rechoice-review.json
python manage.py evaluate_previews --reviews /tmp/rechoice-review.json

Fill in the reviewer name, identify human or AI review, and mark blinding truthfully. Null or not-assessed fields do not count as reviewed. Do not mark a requested difference visible when no comparison request was recorded. Keep the original and corrected render assessments distinct.
